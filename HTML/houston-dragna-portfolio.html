04About me

Hello there

My name is Houston. I design brand systems and then build the best content possible inside them. Key art, identity, layout, and the photography that goes with it.

11 years of it and counting, most recently at Fender, where I've taken work from a global software launch to trade show floors. I'm a photographer by trade and a working audio engineer, so a project can go a long way before I have to hand it off.

Whether it's a launch campaign or snapshots at my daughters' birthday party, I can't help but get invested in the craft. Every image, every layout, every bit of emotion deserves the same care.

I work with kindness, excellence, and a little fun along the way. If that sounds like your bag, let's talk.
