01 The Mark

Logo

The HD monogram: an italic H whose right stem doubles as the spine of a D, with three trailing arcs off the bowl. The arcs are the idea · motion, echo, repeat. Everything else in the system supports that read.

Anatomy

  • The H. Italic, heavy, forward-leaning. Its right stem is shared structure, not decoration.
  • The D. Built off that shared stem. The two letters are one object; they never separate.
  • The arcs. Three, always three. Full color sets them chartreuse, blue, fuchsia · the entire palette in one gesture.
  • Two lockups only. Filled · NO BG (the mark alone) and Box (mark knocked out of a filled plate). No third lockup exists.

Theme pairing

The site runs one variant per theme, everywhere chrome appears: topbar, corner, and all footers. One mark per lockup, never both.

Filled · NO BG · Full ColorDark theme

The primary mark. Lives on Ink Black and any dark ground. This is the default answer to "which logo".

Box · Knockout · Off BlackLight theme

The light-ground mark. The plate gives it an edge the bare mark loses on near-white. Never place it on a dark ground; it disappears.

The full set

Twelve files exist. Colorway variants are for special placements · merch, one-off art, colored grounds · and always sit on a ground that gives the mark at least 3:1 edge contrast.

Filled · SnowSupport

Single-color on dark. For contexts where full color is too loud: watermarks, print over photography.

Filled · BlueSupport

Brand blue single-color. Reads on dark and on snow.

Filled · ChartreuseSupport

Named "Green" in the files; the color is brand chartreuse. Dark grounds only.

Box · Full ColorSupport

The plated full-color lockup. Works on either ground; the site's original topbar mark before theme pairing.

Box · Knockout · SnowSupport

Inverse of the light-theme mark. Dark or colored grounds.

Box · Knockout · BlueSupport

Also in the set: Knockout Red, Knockout Chartreuse, Filled Red, Filled Off Black. Same ground rules apply.

Clearspace & minimum size

½X ½X

Rules

  • Clearspace = half the mark's height on all four sides. Text, edges, and other art stay outside it.
  • Minimum size: 15px tall digital, 6mm print. Below that the arcs fuse into a blob. The smallest shipped use is 15px.
  • Shipped chrome sizes: topbar 17px · corner 26 to 34px · footers 15 to 21px. Stay in that family.
  • Where it does not go: the resume and application documents. The mark stays on the site, client quotes, and client-facing docs.

Misuse

Recolor

No off-palette tints, ever. Use a supplied variant or nothing.

Stretch or squash

The ratio is fixed (roughly 4:3). Scale both axes together or not at all.

Rotate

The italic lean IS the motion. Rotating it fights the drawing.

Effects

No glows, shadows, outlines, or gradients on the mark.

Wrong ground

The off-black knockout on a dark ground vanishes. Match variant to ground, always.

Doubling up

One mark per lockup. Two variants side by side is a bug, not a system.

02 Palette

Five colors. No tints, no invented values, no gradients. Anything that is not one of these five, the dim grey, or an alpha of them, is off-brand. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Ink Black

Fills and dark plates. The page ground is its 25% desaturated sibling, #06070D.

The dark-theme accent. Menus, labels, crosses, primary buttons. Ink Black text on it.

Text on dark, text on blue, text on fuchsia. Never pure white.

Hot Fuchsia

The interruption color: hover states, warnings, one word in a headline. Small doses.

Dim

Secondary text on dark only. Too light to print on white; use #5A5A63 there.

03 Typefaces

Typography

Three faces, three jobs, no exceptions. Display shouts, mono organizes, body explains. No serifs anywhere in the system, and no outlined or decorated display faces, ever.

Display · Archivo Variablewght 900 · wdth 118% · tracking -0.02em · leading 0.86

Command every channel.
Control every stage.

It is not Archivo Black. The system loads the Archivo variable family (wdth 62 to 125, wght 400 to 900) and renders display type at weight 900 with 118% width. Archivo Black is a separate single-weight family with its own drawing. Specifying it anywhere introduces a second, subtly different font. On documents that cannot ship the variable file, Archivo Black is the accepted print stand-in; on the web it is wrong.

04 Dual Ground

Themes

One system, two grounds. Dark is the default and the identity; light is a full first-class citizen, not an inversion filter. The accent changes hands between them: chartreuse carries dark, brand blue carries light.

Frequently asked.

The accent, the crosses, the logo, and the text derivatives all swap together. Nothing is recomputed by filter; every value is a deliberate token.

Interaction language

Hover · color flips, not fades

Dragna

Buttons lift 2px and flip to another palette color. Display-type links cycle the palette per hover. The flip is instant-feeling: 200 to 250ms.

Standing rules

  • Transform and opacity only. Never animate blur or filters on touch devices; never animate layout properties.
  • Type morphs by character roll: letters slide in clipped cells, widths glide with them. 620ms, 34ms per-letter stagger.
  • Reduced motion is honored everywhere: animation collapses, content never disappears.
  • Idle costs nothing. Loops short-circuit when settled; the cross field sleeps until the cursor moves.

06 Grounds & Furniture

Furniture

The recurring props that make a page read as this brand before a single word is set.

The cross field

26px hairline crosses on a 152px grid (128px on phones), every other row offset half a cell so it never reads as graph paper. Chartreuse at 21% on dark, blue at 50% on light. Within 300px of the cursor they push away, lean 6 degrees, and swell 10%. It is the background of this very page.

The coordinate rail

Vertical Space Mono marginalia on both page edges: name and year on the left, 30.4515° N / 91.1871° W on the right. Location is furniture, never a headline; the footer says Available worldwide.

One rhythm token

Every section's top and bottom padding is the same clamp(60px, 6.5vw, 92px) value, so two stacked sections always read as exactly twice it. Bespoke margins are how spacing drifts; there are none.

The chrome band

A fixed 48px lane, 12px from the top: the mark left, the pill right. Radius language everywhere else follows it · 60px nav pill, 44px buttons, 28px panels, 14px cards.

Numbered eyebrows

Sections announce themselves with a chartreuse index chip and a mono label: 06 · Questions. The number is set in the accent; the label stays dim.

Hero discipline

The hero name and morph fill exactly 60% of the viewport width at every size, leading 0.86, sitting over imagery with the meta row pinned beneath. On-image text is snow in both themes.

07 Words

Voice

The writing is part of the identity. Short declaratives, the joke on the second sentence, zero filler.

Rules

  • No em-dashes. Anywhere. Ever. The middot is the house separator: Est. 2015 · 11 yrs. Sentences restructure instead.
  • Labels are uppercase mono. Headlines are uppercase display. Body is sentence case. Title Case is not used.
  • Two short sentences beat one long one. "Second breakfast is served. This page is not."
  • Numbers carry claims. 41 trade press stories, 150+ final images. But client performance data is never published; scope counts only.
